A Key to Reading the Bible

A free, Scripture-centered guide for reading the Bible through love, truth, humility, and freedom of choice.

The Key Is First a Mirror, Not a Weapon

This Key is not another authority telling you what you must believe.

It is a set of questions designed to help us examine what we read, what we are taught, and even what we ourselves believe.

Scripture says. Evidence shows. Interpretation suggests. Speculation explores.

Interesting is not the same as true.

The Key belongs to everyone.

No person becomes the Key. No AI becomes the Key. No organization becomes the Key. No numerical pattern becomes the Key. No interpretation becomes untouchable.

The messenger does not get a pass.

Twelve Tests

1. Text — What does the passage actually say?
2. Context — What surrounds it?
3. Scripture interprets Scripture — Are there genuine internal parallels?
4. Christ — Does the interpretation remain consistent with Jesus’ teaching?
5. Love — Does it preserve love of God and neighbor?
6. Truth — Are facts separated from assumptions?
7. Freedom — Does it avoid coercion?
8. Humility — Can we admit, “We don’t know”?
9. Consistency — Does the rule work in other passages too?
10. No forced numbers — Are numerical patterns discovered rather than manufactured?
11. Fruit — What does the interpretation produce in practice?
12. Return to Scripture — After analysis, does it still stand when we return to the original text?

A Simple Rule

Correct the idea without condemning the person.

We can recognize harm, disagree, establish boundaries, and speak truthfully without turning another human being into the enemy.

Their choice belongs to them. My choice belongs to me.

The Purpose

To help people seek truth without surrendering their freedom, pursue understanding without pride, correct error without hatred, and read Scripture without turning interpretation into a weapon.

Seek. Test. Listen. Examine. Love. Return to Scripture.
